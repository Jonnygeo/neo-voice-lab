> **“Where AI finds your voice — and legacy finds its tone.”**  
> Neo Voice Lab is the experimental voice tuning module within the [NeoLegacy AI Platform](https://github.com/Jonnygeo/NeoLegacy). It’s where emotional tone, pitch, pacing, and message shaping get tested, refined, and embedded into your AI legacy.

---

## 🔬 Features

- 🎙️ ElevenLabs-powered voice modeling
- 🔁 Run text variations to test tone, emotion, and delivery
- 🧠 Optimize messages for child profile, legacy mood, or future date
- 🧪 Sandbox environment to explore TTS without affecting final delivery engine
- 🎯 Outputs: `.mp3`, base64, or stream-ready format

---

## ⚙️ Tech Stack

- Node.js
- ElevenLabs TTS SDK
- JSON-based message and profile structure
- Optional: Firestore for saving lab presets
